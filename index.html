As Director of WKS Africa, Wade Hammond is dedicated to developing the next generation of African combat sports athletes. His mission is to create pathways for talented fighters across the continent to compete on the world stage.

Through strategic partnerships, athlete development programs, and high-profile events like the World Grand Prix, WKS Africa is establishing Africa as a powerhouse in global kickboxing.
